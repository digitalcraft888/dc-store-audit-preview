Is the free store review really free?+

Yes, free and no-obligation. You get a senior-led review of your live store, the three highest-impact fixes, and a clear next step, usually within one business day.

Can you build a new store from scratch, or migrate me to Shopify?+

Yes. We design and build new Shopify stores, including Shopify Plus, and can plan a move from another platform. We start with your catalogue, brand and goals, then agree scope before development.

Do you work on Shopify and Shopify Plus?+

Yes. We're a Shopify Plus partner and work across both, from theme builds to custom functionality, subscriptions, B2B and integrations.

Do I own the work you build?+

Completely. You own all code, design files and accounts, with no lock-in and optional ongoing support.

How long does a build take, and what does it cost?+

Most builds run four to ten weeks depending on scope. After the audit we send a clear, itemised proposal, no surprise costs.
